⚡ Pixel Sandbox Simulator

Your world begins as an empty canvas. This world is yours — shape the land, breathe life into it, and watch civilizations rise and fall.

How to play: pick one of Your Powers on the left, then click the world. Paint Grass & Trees to make land habitable, drop Humans or Elves, then press ▶ Play and watch villages grow into kingdoms. Unleash Disasters to test them.
Play As: tired of watching? Drop in as a Villager, Knight, Archmage, Musketeer, Elf Ranger, Warchief, Revenant or Great Beast — or Possess anyone already alive — and walk your world yourself.
